Premium CBS
Core Banking Solution

A fully integrated, real-time, multi-branch core banking platform purpose-built for Nepal's cooperatives, microfinances, and credit unions. Covering every function from member onboarding to NCRA regulatory reporting — in one system.

Features

Built for every function
of your institution.

💰
Fixed & Recurring Deposits
Configure FD and RD products with custom tenures, interest rates, and auto-renewal rules. Interest calculation is automatic and posted to GL in real time.
🏦
Savings Account Management
Multiple savings account types — mandatory, voluntary, children's, and group savings — each with configurable interest slabs and withdrawal limits.
📊
Share Capital Management
Track member share purchases, transfers, refunds, and dividend distribution. Supports cooperative and SACCOS share structures as per Nepal Cooperative Act.
🔄
Inter-Account Transfers
Real-time fund transfers between member accounts, branches, or to external banks. Full transaction history with timestamps and teller IDs.
📅
Auto-Interest Posting
Scheduled daily, monthly, or quarterly interest posting with automatic GL entries. No manual journal entries required for routine interest accrual.
🧾
Passbook & Statement
Print-ready passbook templates and digital statements with full transaction history. Supports both English and Nepali printing.
📋
Loan Application & Appraisal
Digital loan application capture with customizable appraisal checklists. Supports individual and group loans, including agriculture, business, and personal loan types.
📈
EMI Schedule Generation
Automatic EMI calculation with flat, diminishing balance, or equal principal methods. Generate loan schedules instantly and email to members.
⚠️
Overdue & NPA Tracking
Real-time overdue loan tracking with aging buckets (0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days). NPA classification follows NCRA directives automatically.
🔗
Collateral Management
Record and track collateral against loans — property, gold, FD lien, guarantor details. Collateral revaluation schedules and expiry alerts included.
💳
Partial Payment & Prepayment
Accept partial EMI payments, advance payments, and full prepayments with automatic recalculation of remaining schedule and interest rebate.
📝
Loan Document Generation
Auto-generate sanction letters, agreement templates, demand notices, and repayment cards. Fully customizable with your institution's letterhead.
📒
General Ledger
Full double-entry GL with multi-level chart of accounts. Every banking transaction auto-posts to the appropriate GL account without manual entries.
🏢
Multi-Branch Consolidation
Consolidate financials from all branches in real time. View branch-wise P&L, balance sheet, and trial balance from head office.
📅
Day-End & Month-End Close
Structured EOD process with automated balance checks, interest posting, and provision entries. Month-end close generates financial statements automatically.
💱
Expense & Income Vouchers
Record operational expenses and non-banking income with department and cost-center coding. Approval workflows for expenses above configurable thresholds.
📊
Financial Statements
Auto-generated Balance Sheet, Income Statement, Trial Balance, and Cash Flow Statement — NCRA and cooperative act format ready.
🔍
Full Audit Trail
Every transaction, modification, and reversal is logged with user ID, timestamp, and before/after values. Tamper-proof and available for regulatory inspection.
👤
Member Onboarding & KYC
Complete digital member registration with KYC document capture, photo, signature, and citizenship scan. Supports individual, joint, and group member types.
🆔
Member ID & Account Linking
Unique member IDs linked to all their accounts, loans, shares, and transaction history. Full 360° member view in one screen.
👨‍👩‍👧
Group & Committee Management
Manage member groups, committees, and boards. Track group savings, group loans, and attendance records for group meetings.
📞
Member Communication Log
Record calls, visits, and notices against member profiles. Automated SMS and email alerts for dues, maturity, and announcements.
🔒
Role-Based Access Control
Define user roles — teller, supervisor, manager, auditor — with granular permission sets. No user can access what they're not authorized to see or modify.
📍
Branch & Department Setup
Configure unlimited branches, sub-branches, and departments with individual bank accounts, staff assignments, and independent operating limits.
🏛️
NCRA Regulatory Returns
Auto-generate NCRA quarterly and annual returns in the prescribed formats. Deposit and loan return templates updated to match current NCRA circulars.
📊
MIS Dashboard
Live management information system with branch-wise performance, loan portfolio health, deposit growth, and member activity — updated in real time.
📉
Portfolio Quality Reports
PAR (Portfolio at Risk) ratios by product, branch, and loan officer. Provision requirement calculations and write-off schedules following NCRA guidelines.
🧮
Custom Report Builder
Build your own reports from any data point in CBS — select fields, apply filters, group by branch or product, and export to Excel or PDF.
📆
Scheduled Report Delivery
Set up automated daily, weekly, or monthly reports to be emailed to management and board members — no manual generation needed.
🔎
Internal Audit Reports
Transaction exception reports, teller reconciliation, and dormant account summaries designed for internal and external audit support.

How it works

From CBS to the SCT network.

1
Link Premium CBS to SCT
The core is already built to sit with SCT. We connect your institution so card activity, settlement, and member accounts share one platform.
2
Issue the chip card
Staff issue an SCT debit or prepaid chip card. Members then reach ATMs, POS, ecommerce, and mobile commerce. You can also deploy your own ATM or POS.
3
Settle and resolve in SCT's tools
Daily reports and settlement come from the SCT Report Portal. Member requests go through CRMS. Disputed transactions go through DMS, the dispute management system.

How it works

📋 Collections · Slip on the spot · Wi-Fi sync

Tablet Banking
replaces the collection sheet.

Marketing representatives used to carry a collection sheet and could post the amount in the software only after they reached the office. That delay brought wrong account numbers, wrong amounts, slow posting, misplaced sheets, and irregular records. Other organizations built mobile apps that only replaced the sheet. Ours records the collection easily, reduces the chance a transaction is missed or posted wrongly, and prints the deposit slip on the spot while the collector posts it — so both the institution and the member can rely on it.

Features

Collect, print, and post
without the paper sheet.

💵
Deposit collections
The representative records the deposit against the member account while they are with the member. The amount is not copied from a sheet back at the office, where an account number or a figure is easy to mistype.
📘
Deposit and loan product information

📋
Loan collections
Loan installments are collected on the same visit. The repayment is posted to the loan, not held on paper until someone at the branch finds time to enter it.

🖨️
Bill printing from a POS
The collector prints the deposit slip on a POS device at the moment the transaction is posted. The member keeps the slip, and the institution keeps the electronic entry.
How it works

Post with the member. Sync at the office.

1
Open the account on the device
The representative selects the member and the product — deposit, loan, or fee — instead of writing an account number on a collection sheet.
2
Post and print the slip together
The amount is posted while the collector is still there, and the POS prints the deposit slip on the spot. The member can check the account and the figure before the representative leaves.
3
Sync over office Wi-Fi
When the device joins the office Wi-Fi, the day's collections land in Premium CBS. Nobody retypes them from paper, Excel, or a USB drive.

We built Nepal's cooperative banking software from the ground up.

Premium Technologies was founded by a team of software engineers who saw firsthand how Nepal's cooperatives and microfinances were running on spreadsheets, paper ledgers, and outdated systems. We set out to change that — and have spent 12 years doing exactly that.

Our Story

12 years, 500+ institutions, one mission.

In 2015, three engineers from Kathmandu University sat in a small office in Tinkune and wrote the first version of Premium CBS. At the time, most cooperatives in Nepal managed their members' savings in physical registers. Loan tracking was done in Excel. NCRA reporting was a quarterly nightmare.

"We didn't want to build a generic banking system and adapt it for Nepal. We wanted to build Nepal's banking system from scratch."

We started with a single cooperative in Kathmandu. Within three years, we had 50 clients across 4 provinces. Today, Premium Technologies is deployed in 500+ institutions across all 7 provinces, from Koshi to Sudurpashchim — including some of Nepal's largest cooperatives and growing microfinances.

Every feature in the system started as feedback from a client. We still answer our own phones. We still visit clients on-site when something is wrong. That hasn't changed in 12 years, and we don't plan to change it.

FAQ

Common questions.

How long does implementation take? +
Most institutions are live within 2–4 weeks. This includes data migration from your existing system, system configuration, and staff training. Larger institutions with many branches may take 4–8 weeks. We handle the entire process — you don't need a dedicated IT team on your side.
Can you migrate our existing data? +
Yes. We have migrated data from Excel spreadsheets, legacy DOS-based systems, and other CBS platforms. Our team will assess your current data format and handle the migration. Member records, account balances, loan schedules, and transaction history can all be brought across.
What does the pricing look like? +
Pricing is based on institution size, number of branches, and the products you select. We offer both one-time license and annual subscription models. Contact our sales team for a quote tailored to your institution — we don't publish fixed prices because every institution's needs are different.
Does the system work without internet? +
Premium CBS is web-based and requires an internet or local network connection for the main system. However, Tablet Banking is offline-capable — field officers can collect deposits and record transactions without connectivity, and data syncs when they return to a connected area. SMS Banking also works without internet for members.
What kind of support do you provide after go-live? +
All clients receive ongoing support via phone and email. Our team is based in Kathmandu and can visit on-site when needed. Existing clients also get access to our support portal and can raise tickets that are tracked until resolution. Critical issues are escalated immediately and typically resolved same-day.
Is Premium CBS compliant with the latest NCRA directives? +
Yes. We actively monitor NCRA circulars and directives and release updates to keep the system compliant. When NCRA issues new reporting requirements or changes to cooperative regulations, we update the system and notify all clients. You don't need to track regulatory changes yourself.
