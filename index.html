How Premium CBS Connects Your Financial Ecosystem

Premium CBS is designed as the central technology platform for modern financial institutions, connecting every banking channel and operational service through a secure, real-time, and unified ecosystem.

From branch operations to digital banking services, every transaction flows through a single reliable core engine, ensuring consistency, accuracy, security, and seamless connectivity across all channels.

Real-Time Core Banking Engine

At the center of the ecosystem is the Premium CBS Core Engine, which acts as a single source of truth for all financial transactions. All customer-facing channels and operational modules communicate with the core system to ensure real-time processing and accurate data synchronization.

Premium CBS connects to the fintech services your members expect. We have already integrated SCT ATM services for our clients. If your institution wants to offer ATM service, the platform is ready and tested.

Features

Cards, ATMs, POS,
and the reports behind them.

💳
SCT debit chip card
Issue an SCT debit chip card against the member account in Premium CBS, so cash withdrawal is a core transaction and not a separate register.
🏧
Nationwide ATM network
Members reach the SCT ATM network used across Nepal by banks, finances, and cooperatives. Your institution does not have to build a switch of its own.
📊
SCT Report Portal
Open the SCT report portal for the daily report and settlement, so card activity and the CBS ledger can be matched the same day.
🗂️
Customer Request Management
SCT CRMS is the customer request management system. Card requests, replacements, and member follow-ups stay in one place instead of on paper at the branch.
🏦
Deploy your own ATM
Place an ATM at your office or a high-traffic point. It sits on the same SCT connection already linked to Premium CBS.
🎫
SCT prepaid chip card
Issue a prepaid chip card when a full deposit account is not the right product, and still keep issuance inside the institution's control.
🛍️
POS, ecommerce, and mobile commerce
The same card reaches the SCT POS network, ecommerce, and mobile commerce, so members can pay beyond the ATM.
⚖️
Dispute Management System
SCT DMS is the dispute management system. Questioned ATM or POS transactions are raised and tracked there, with the CBS entry available for the amount and the account.
🖨️
Deploy your own POS
Run your own point-of-sale device for card acceptance at the counter or with a field team, on the SCT POS network.
How it works

From CBS to the SCT network.

1
Link Premium CBS to SCT
The core is already built to sit with SCT. We connect your institution so card activity, settlement, and member accounts share one platform.
2
Issue the chip card
Staff issue an SCT debit or prepaid chip card. Members then reach ATMs, POS, ecommerce, and mobile commerce. You can also deploy your own ATM or POS.
3
Settle and resolve in SCT's tools
Daily reports and settlement come from the SCT Report Portal. Member requests go through CRMS. Disputed transactions go through DMS, the dispute management system.

How it works

From the ledger to the handset.

Use the number already on the member

📋 Field collection · Real-time recording

Premium Mobile Collector

Smart Digital Collection Solution for Field Operations

Premium Mobile Collector is a powerful digital collection application designed to simplify and automate field collection activities of cooperatives and financial institutions.

Traditionally, field representatives relied on manual collection sheets, causing delays, data entry errors, incorrect account details, and challenges in maintaining accurate records. Premium Mobile Collector eliminates these limitations by enabling real-time transaction recording, reducing operational risks, and improving collection efficiency.

With an easy-to-use mobile interface, collectors can securely record transactions, synchronize data directly with the banking system, and provide members with instant deposit and loan collection receipts at the time of collection.

How it works

Record it in the field.
The member gets the receipt.

📝
Real-time transaction recording
Collectors record transactions as they happen, instead of writing them on a collection sheet and entering them later. That removes the delays, data entry errors, and incorrect account details that come with manual sheets.
🔄
Direct sync with the banking system
Collection data synchronizes directly with the banking system, so the institution keeps an accurate record without retyping from paper.
🧾
Instant deposit & loan collection receipts
Members receive a deposit & loan receipt at the time of collection, while the collector is still with them.
💳
Loan collection receipts
When a collector takes a loan installment, the member gets a receipt showing the amount paid, the principal and interest split, the remaining balance, and the next due date.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

About the Product
What is Premium CBS? +

Premium CBS is a cloud-based Core Banking Solution designed for cooperatives, microfinance institutions, and financial organizations. It helps institutions manage their complete banking operations through a secure, integrated, and efficient digital platform.

What services are included with Premium CBS? +

Premium CBS provides a complete banking ecosystem with integrated modules and services, including:

  • Customer & Member Management
  • Deposit and Savings Management
  • Loan Management
  • Accounting and Reporting
  • SMS Banking
  • Mobile Banking
  • ATM Banking
  • Mobile Collector
  • Third-Party Integrations
Can Premium CBS support multiple branches? +

Yes. Premium CBS supports multi-branch operations, allowing institutions to manage multiple locations through a centralized banking platform with consistent data, real-time transaction processing, and efficient branch management.

How secure is Premium CBS? +

Premium CBS is built with a strong focus on security and reliability to protect financial data and transactions. The system includes:

  • Secure digital certificates
  • Role-based access control
  • Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
  • Reliable database management
  • Real-time database replication
  • Continuous system monitoring by our expert technical team

These security measures help ensure safe, reliable, and uninterrupted banking operations.

Can Premium CBS be customized according to institutional requirements? +

Yes. Premium CBS can be customized based on your institution’s operational workflows, reporting requirements, and specific business needs. Our team works closely with institutions to configure solutions that best fit their processes.

Is Premium CBS compliant with the latest NCRA directives? +

Yes. We continuously monitor NCRA circulars and regulatory directives to keep Premium CBS aligned with changing requirements.

Whenever new reporting requirements or regulatory updates are introduced, we enhance the system and notify our clients, helping institutions stay compliant without having to manage regulatory changes independently.

Migration & Implementation
How does the migration process from an existing software system to Premium CBS work? +

Premium Technologies follows a structured and secure migration process to ensure a smooth transition from your existing banking software to Premium CBS.

Our migration team works closely with your institution to understand your current system, prepare a migration plan, validate data, and ensure minimal operational disruption.

The migration process includes:

  1. System Assessment & PlanningWe analyze your existing software, database structure, available data, and institutional requirements to create a customized migration plan.
  2. Data Extraction & TransformationExisting data is securely extracted from your current system and transformed into a format compatible with Premium CBS while maintaining accuracy and data integrity.
  3. Data Validation & VerificationMigrated data is carefully verified to ensure customer records, accounts, deposits, loans, transactions, and other critical information are transferred accurately.
  4. Testing & User TrainingBefore going live, we perform migration testing and provide necessary training to your team to ensure smooth system adoption.
  5. Go-Live & Post-Migration SupportAfter successful validation, Premium CBS is deployed for daily operations with continuous support from our technical team.
Can existing data from our current software be migrated to Premium CBS? +

Yes. Existing data from your current banking software can be migrated to Premium CBS. Our team assists institutions in transferring essential information while maintaining data accuracy, security, and business continuity.

The migration may include:

  • Customer and member records
  • Deposit and savings accounts
  • Loan accounts and repayment details
  • Transaction history
  • Share information
  • Required operational data
Will there be any disruption during the migration process? +

Our migration process is carefully planned to minimize operational disruption. We coordinate with your institution, schedule migration activities, perform necessary validations, and ensure a smooth transition before the final go-live.

How long does implementation take? +

Implementation timelines depend on the institution’s size, operational requirements, data migration needs, and customization scope. Our team works closely with each institution throughout the implementation, testing, and go-live process.
