of your institution.

Fixed & Recurring Deposits

Share Capital Management
Track member share purchases, transfers, refunds, and dividend distribution. Supports cooperative and SACCOS share structures as per Nepal Cooperative Act.
🔄
Inter-Account Transfers
Real-time fund transfers between member accounts, branches, or to external banks. Full transaction history with timestamps and teller IDs.
📅
Auto-Interest Posting
Scheduled daily, monthly, or quarterly interest posting with automatic GL entries. No manual journal entries required for routine interest accrual.
🧾
Passbook & Statement
Print-ready passbook templates and digital statements with full transaction history. Supports both English and Nepali printing.

Loan Application & Appraisal
Digital loan application capture with customizable appraisal checklists. Supports individual and group loans, including agriculture, business, and personal loan types.
📈
EMI Schedule Generation
Automatic EMI calculation with flat, diminishing balance, or equal principal methods. Generate loan schedules instantly and email to members.
⚠️
Overdue & NPA Tracking
Real-time overdue loan tracking with aging buckets (0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days). NPA classification follows NRB directives automatically.
🔗
Collateral Management
Record and track collateral against loans — property, gold, FD lien, guarantor details. Collateral revaluation schedules and expiry alerts included.
💳
Partial Payment & Prepayment
Accept partial EMI payments, advance payments, and full prepayments with automatic recalculation of remaining schedule and interest rebate.
📝
Loan Document Generation
Auto-generate sanction letters, agreement templates, demand notices, and repayment cards. Fully customizable with your institution's letterhead.

General Ledger
Full double-entry GL with multi-level chart of accounts. Every banking transaction auto-posts to the appropriate GL account without manual entries.

Multi-Branch Consolidation

Day-End & Month-End Close

Expense & Income Vouchers
Record operational expenses and non-banking income with department and cost-center coding. Approval workflows for expenses above configurable thresholds.
📊
Financial Statements
Auto-generated Balance Sheet, Income Statement, Trial Balance, and Cash Flow Statement — NRB and cooperative act format ready.
🔍
Full Audit Trail
Every transaction, modification, and reversal is logged with user ID, timestamp, and before/after values. Tamper-proof and available for regulatory inspection.

Member Onboarding & KYC

Member ID & Account Linking

The collection is posted
before the representative leaves.

Deposit collections

Loan collections

Bill printing from a POS

How it works

Record it once. The member sees the slip.

1
Select the member and the product
The representative opens the account and the deposit, loan, or fee product on the phone. Nothing is written on a collection sheet to be interpreted later.
2
Post and print together
The amount is posted in front of the member and the POS prints the deposit slip immediately. Both sides can see that the account and the amount match.
3
Sync on the office Wi-Fi
The day's collections move into Premium CBS over Wi-Fi. Staff do not export a spreadsheet or join a USB stick to a computer.

We built Nepal's cooperative banking software from the ground up.

Premium Technologies was founded by a team of software engineers who saw firsthand how Nepal's cooperatives and microfinances were running on spreadsheets, paper ledgers, and outdated systems. We set out to change that — and have spent 12 years doing exactly that.

Our Story

12 years, 450+ institutions, one mission.

In 2015, three engineers from Kathmandu University sat in a small office in Tinkune and wrote the first version of Premium CBS. At the time, most cooperatives in Nepal managed their members' savings in physical registers. Loan tracking was done in Excel. NRB reporting was a quarterly nightmare.

"We didn't want to build a generic banking system and adapt it for Nepal. We wanted to build Nepal's banking system from scratch."

We started with a single cooperative in Kathmandu. Within three years, we had 50 clients across 4 provinces. Today, Premium Technologies is deployed in 450+ institutions across all 7 provinces, from Koshi to Sudurpashchim — including some of Nepal's largest cooperatives and growing microfinances.

Every feature in the system started as feedback from a client. We still answer our own phones. We still visit clients on-site when something is wrong. That hasn't changed in 12 years, and we don't plan to change it.

FAQ

Common questions.

How long does implementation take? +
Most institutions are live within 2–4 weeks. This includes data migration from your existing system, system configuration, and staff training. Larger institutions with many branches may take 4–8 weeks. We handle the entire process — you don't need a dedicated IT team on your side.
Can you migrate our existing data? +
Yes. We have migrated data from Excel spreadsheets, legacy DOS-based systems, and other CBS platforms. Our team will assess your current data format and handle the migration. Member records, account balances, loan schedules, and transaction history can all be brought across.
What does the pricing look like? +
Pricing is based on institution size, number of branches, and the products you select. We offer both one-time license and annual subscription models. Contact our sales team for a quote tailored to your institution — we don't publish fixed prices because every institution's needs are different.
Does the system work without internet? +
Premium CBS is web-based and requires an internet or local network connection for the main system. However, Tablet Banking is offline-capable — field officers can collect deposits and record transactions without connectivity, and data syncs when they return to a connected area. SMS Banking also works without internet for members.
What kind of support do you provide after go-live? +
All clients receive ongoing support via phone and email. Our team is based in Kathmandu and can visit on-site when needed. Existing clients also get access to our support portal and can raise tickets that are tracked until resolution. Critical issues are escalated immediately and typically resolved same-day.
Is Premium CBS compliant with the latest NRB directives? +
Yes. We actively monitor NRB circulars and directives and release updates to keep the system compliant. When NRB issues new reporting requirements or changes to cooperative regulations, we update the system and notify all clients. You don't need to track regulatory changes yourself.
